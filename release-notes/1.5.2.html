Crema 1.5.2

A maintenance release: fixes across the HUD pipeline, and wording polish in both languages.

Fixed

  • Dragging a brightness bar backed by BetterDisplay is more trustworthy: the bar only anchors to levels confirmed on the wire, and if the channel fails mid-drag the fallback now writes the exact level your finger stopped at — never one it passed through.
  • When Crema hands a key back to the system, its own bar now stands down too — one keypress, one indicator, in every case.
  • A failed mute read during volume-up no longer leaves the Mac muted while the bar keeps rising.
  • Clicks now land exactly on the visible surface while it appears, matching hover — clicks in the dead margin no longer fall through.
  • A completed hover gesture no longer re-routes a later interaction in styles that commit immediately.

Polish

  • Portuguese (Brazil) strings revised to match the system's own register. The three style names — Notch, Card, Classic — are product names and stay in English in both languages.
  • Small English wording fixes in the menu and Settings.

Requirements: macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later · Apple Silicon or Intel.
