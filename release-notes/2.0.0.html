Crema 2.0

A new Crema: a new icon, a Card drawn in the system's own glass, a volume indicator that knows where the sound goes, and a welcome that shows the app working before it asks for anything.

New

  • A new icon. A disc of crema cut by the wave of a pour, drawn in the system's glass on macOS 26 and later.
  • The Card in Liquid Glass. On macOS 26 and later the Card is glass, like the system's volume indicator: pale over a bright page, dark over a dark one, with a thin light edge. The player's glass is darker, so its title and artist read on any page.
  • The Card's indicator, where you want it. Eight places, picked from a menu that shows the real indicator where it will appear. On the sides it stands upright, and at the bottom it stays clear of the Dock.
  • Where the sound goes. The volume indicator shows AirPods, headphones, an AirPlay speaker or a display when that is where the sound plays.
  • A welcome that shows Crema working. Five short steps: the real player on your screen, the one permission Crema uses and why, a style to pick, a key to try, and where Crema lives in the menu bar.
  • Long titles scroll in the expanded player instead of being cut short.
  • A clearer menu and Settings. The menu bar menu is grouped under titles, and each display in Settings shows a small picture of what it draws.
  • BetterDisplay without doubles. While Crema replaces the system's indicators, it also takes over BetterDisplay's own, so one key shows one indicator. Quit Crema and BetterDisplay's comes back.

Fixed

  • An indicator under a pointer that isn't moving leaves on time; only a moving pointer holds it.
  • Dragging an indicator's bar applies the levels in the order you dragged them.
  • The Notch style sits exactly on the notch.
  • Surfaces fade out as one piece, with no outline left behind the content.
  • Text and symbols on every surface are easier to read, and the cover no longer flashes a placeholder when the player opens or follows an indicator.

Every word in the app was rewritten, in English and in Portuguese, to say plainly what each thing does.

Requirements: macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later · Apple Silicon or Intel.

Crema isn't notarized by Apple, so a fresh install needs one extra step on first launch — see Install in the README. Updating from 1.2.0 or later happens on its own.
