**Crema 1.5.0**

Styles grew up in this one: every display can now have its own, and choosing one
finally looks like what you get.

**New**

- **Per-display styles.** Declare a style for all displays and override it per
  display, right in Settings › General — one row per connected screen, live as
  you plug and unplug. A display without a notch renders _notch_ as _card_, and
  the controls are honest about it.
- **A welcome tour on first launch.** Five short steps that actually configure
  the app — style, replacing the system indicators, Accessibility, launch at
  login. Every step is skippable; nothing blocks.
- **Style tiles you can read.** The pickers draw each style on your own
  wallpaper, with the surface's miniature content and a one-line caption — and
  the Card indicator (line or filled) is now picked from drawn previews right
  under the style tiles, offered whenever a display renders Card.
- **A quieter, clearer menu.** Transport controls appear only while something is
  playing; status lines say something only when there is something to say.
- **A finer border on the floating styles.** Card and Classic wear a darkened
  rim with a top highlight — visible on bright wallpapers, where the old
  hairline vanished.
- **Low Power Mode is respected.** The now-playing waveform freezes while your
  Mac is saving energy, and resumes when it stops.

**Hardened**

- The update feed is now signed end to end: the app verifies the appcast itself
  and every update before extraction.
- When an update is waiting, the menu bar says so — with a button that takes you
  to it.

**Requirements:** macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later · Apple Silicon or Intel.

**Install:** download `Crema.dmg` below, open it, and drag Crema into
Applications. Crema isn't notarized by Apple, so the first launch needs one
extra step — see **First launch** in the README:
https://github.com/colatte/crema#first-launch

Updating from 1.2.0 or later: Crema updates itself — this release will be
offered automatically.
