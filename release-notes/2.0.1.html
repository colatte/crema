Crema 2.0.1

A small fix release. Nothing about how the app works or is set up changed.

Fixed

  • The waveform keeps pulsing. With the Card on screen, a track change could leave the four bars standing still at their peak until playback was paused and resumed, and after the player tucked away while playing, one bar could drop out of sight. The bars now keep their pulse through both.

Requirements: macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later · Apple Silicon or Intel.

Crema isn't notarized by Apple, so a fresh install needs one extra step on first launch — see Install in the README. Updating from 1.2.0 or later happens on its own.
