Blends into the notch

Black on black, flush with the bezel. A new track shows a sliver, its title and a waveform, then tucks away. Click the notch and the player opens.

Indicators

Indicators that match. Keys that behave.

Turn on one switch and Crema replaces the system's volume, brightness and keyboard backlight indicators with its own, in the style you picked. It stays off until you choose it, and quitting Crema brings the system's back.

Honest with your keys

A key Crema can't act on, a display it doesn't drive or a device with no volume, goes back to the system whole, indicator and all.

Follows your pointer

A brightness key acts on the display under your pointer. Crema drives the built-in one; any other gets the key back, so macOS shows its own.

Recovers on its own

If one channel fails mid-press, only that one steps aside, and it comes back when the device does. The menu bar tells you if it doesn't.

Works with BetterDisplay

With BetterDisplay 4.2.1 or newer, Crema shows the brightness indicator for an external display, on the screen it belongs to.

Card

The indicator goes where you look.

On Card, the volume and brightness indicator can sit in one of eight places, as a thin line or filling the whole card. At the bottom it stays clear of the Dock.

Details

A touch of its colour

The player borrows a hint of the colour in the artwork that's playing. A touch, never a wash.

Notch on the built-in display, Card on an external one. The player shows only on the displays you switch on.

Install

Three steps, and one honest warning.

Download the disk image

One file, from GitHub Releases.

Drag Crema into Applications

It lives in the menu bar, not the Dock.

Follow the welcome tour

It shows Crema working, asks for Accessibility so it can notice the volume and brightness keys, and lets you pick a style. Every step can be skipped.

The first time you open it

Crema isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS warns about an unidentified developer. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway, once. If there's no button, run this in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Crema.app
